Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

The short version: your swing videos never leave your phone. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking. An account is optional; if you create one, we store your email, your name if you give one, and your practice stats so they sync between devices. You can delete your account and that data at any time from inside the app.

This policy explains what information the 3to1 Golf app and website (“3to1 Golf”, “we”, “us”) collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We're based in Australia and handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Using the app without an account

You can use 3to1 Golf without signing up. Everything you do — tempo settings, swing videos you load, markers, practice history — is stored only on your device. We don't receive any of it.

Information we collect if you create an account

  • Account details: your email address, your name (optional), and your password. We never see or store your password itself — only a one-way cryptographic hash of it.
  • Practice stats: for each day you practice, the date, time practiced and number of swings analyzed. For each swing you analyze: the date and time, the measured tempo ratio and accuracy score, long or short game, the club category, and the golfer label shown on that swing (for example, a name you typed).
  • Password reset codes: if you use “Forgot Password”, we create a one-time code that expires after 15 minutes. We store only a hash of it and delete it once used.

Information we don't collect

  • Videos and images. Swing videos and thumbnails stay on your device and are never uploaded to us.
  • Location. The app doesn't access your location.
  • Microphone or camera. The app plays audio but never records it, and it doesn't use your camera. It picks existing videos from your library only when you choose one.
  • Advertising, analytics or tracking data. The app contains no ad networks, analytics tools or tracking technology, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Payment information. 3to1 Golf doesn't take payments.

Permissions the app asks for

Photos / videos: so you can pick a swing video to analyze. The video is read on your device and isn't uploaded. You can change this at any time in your phone's settings.

How we use your information

  • To create your account, sign you in, and keep you signed in.
  • To sync your practice stats between your devices and keep them if you reinstall the app.
  • To send password reset codes you request. We don't send marketing email.
  • To keep the service secure and working, for example by limiting repeated password-reset attempts.

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run 3to1 Golf. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Cloud hosting and database providers, which run our server and store account data. Like most online services, they may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability.
  • Resend, which delivers password reset emails and so processes your email address and the email's contents.

These providers may store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

If you tap “Watch on YouTube” in the app, YouTube opens and its own privacy policy applies. Apple and Google handle app downloads under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep account data until you delete your account. When you do, your account and all synced practice stats are removed from our database straight away. Copies may remain in our providers' routine backups for a limited time before being overwritten. Password reset codes expire after 15 minutes and are deleted once used.

Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords and reset codes are stored only as hashes, and sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see your stats in the app and change your name under Profile → Edit Profile. Email us to request a copy of what we hold or to correct anything else.
  • Deletion: delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete Account, or see how to request deletion.
  • Stop syncing: sign out and the app keeps working with data stored only on your device.

If you have a privacy complaint, contact us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Children

3to1 Golf isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we'll let you know in the app.
