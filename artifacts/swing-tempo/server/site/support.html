Frequently asked questions

What does 3:1 mean?

It's the ratio between your backswing time and your downswing time. Many tour players take about three times as long to swing back as they do to swing down, whatever their overall speed. Short-game shots tend to be closer to 2:1, which is why the app has both.

What do tempos like 21/7 mean?

They're video frame counts at 30 frames per second: 21 frames of backswing and 7 of downswing. Every full-swing preset is 3:1; lower numbers are quicker, higher numbers are smoother.

I can't hear the tones

Check your media volume, and make sure Bluetooth headphones are connected if you're using them. On iPhone the tones play even with the ring/silent switch on silent.

How do I measure my own swing's tempo?

Film your swing, open it in the Analysis tab, and mark takeaway, top and impact. The app works out your backswing-to-downswing ratio from those three points. Filming at a higher frame rate (such as slow-motion) gives more precise results.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. An account backs up your practice stats and syncs them between devices. Your videos always stay on your phone.

I forgot my password

On the Profile tab, tap Forgot Password? and enter your email. We'll send a 6-digit code that's valid for 15 minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm with your password. You can also request deletion by email.
