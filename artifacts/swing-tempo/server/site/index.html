Hear it. Swing it. See it.

Tempo is easier to feel than to think about. Train it by ear, then check it on video.

Hear it

Pick a tempo and the app plays three tones: start back, top of the swing, impact. Use beeps or a spoken cue.

Swing it

Swing to the tones at the range or at home. Film a swing on your phone whenever you want to check it.

See it

Load the video, mark takeaway, top and impact, and get your real backswing-to-downswing ratio.

Everything you need to build a repeatable rhythm

Tempo tones

Five 3:1 tempos for full swings and six 2:1 tempos for chips, pitches and putts, from smooth to tour-quick.

Frame-by-frame analysis

Scrub through your swing, mark the key positions, and replay impact in slow motion.

Pro reference tempos

See the ratios of famous swings and practice to a pro's rhythm instead of a preset.

Ball tracker

Trace the ball's flight on your swing video for a launch-monitor style view of every shot.

Practice tracking

Practice time, swings analyzed and your daily streak, with a year-long activity grid.

Your videos stay yours

Swing videos never leave your phone. No ads, no tracking. An account is optional and only syncs your stats.
