Delete your 3to1 Golf account

You can delete your account at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open 3to1 Golf and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to Settings and tap Delete Account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete.

Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can't access the app, email SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and password hash.
  • All practice stats synced to your account: practice days, swing ratios and accuracy scores.
  • Any pending password reset codes.

This is permanent and can't be undone. Copies may remain in our hosting providers' routine backups for a limited time before being overwritten.

What stays on your phone

Swing videos and anything stored only on your device aren't affected, because we never had them. To remove those too, delete the app from your phone.
